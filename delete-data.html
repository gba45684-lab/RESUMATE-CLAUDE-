Delete your data

ResuMate: Resume Builder, by Hightech Solutions. ResuMate has no accounts, so there is no account to close. Your data is stored on your phone, and you can delete it yourself at any time.

How to delete your data

  1. Open ResuMate and go to Profile, then Data & backup.
  2. Tap Delete all data…, then Yes, delete everything. This removes every resume, download, setting, and saved AI result from your phone. It cannot be undone, so save a backup first if you need one.
  3. To remove the app completely, uninstall it from Android Settings, or long-press the app icon and choose Uninstall. Uninstalling also removes everything ResuMate stored on the phone.

Requesting deletion by email

If you cannot use the app, or you want us to confirm that nothing else is held, email hightechsolutions010@gmail.com from the address you want us to check, with the subject Data deletion request. We will reply within 30 days and confirm what we hold. We hold no resume data on our servers, so the main action is the in-app step above.

Retention

Data stays on your phone until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not keep copies of your resumes on any server.
