ResuMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Summary

ResuMate is a resume builder. Your resumes are stored on your device. We do not run accounts, and we do not sell or share your personal data for advertising.

What is stored on your device

Your resumes, cover letters, job descriptions, downloaded files and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them. If you use the backup feature, the backup file is saved where you choose.

AI suggestions

When you tap an AI feature, the text needed for that request (your resume and the job description you chose to include) is sent through our free AI service. The service forwards it to an AI provider: Google Gemini first, then Groq or Cloudflare Workers AI if the first is busy. The provider processes the text to create the suggestion.

  • Our service does not store or log resume text. It keeps only a random device identifier and a daily count of runs, so the free daily limit can be enforced.
  • Google's free API terms allow Google to use content sent to unpaid services to improve its products. Groq and Cloudflare have their own data terms. Please read them before sending sensitive information.
  • Do not use AI features for information you are not willing to share with these providers.

Update checks

The app checks for updates by sending the app's identifier and installed version to our update service. It does not send your resume or any personal information.

Hosting logs

Our hosting providers (Vercel for update checks, Cloudflare for the AI service, GitHub for app releases) may keep standard server logs, which can include IP addresses, for a short time for security and operation.

Children

ResuMate is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

You can delete your resumes and downloads inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your device. You can avoid AI features altogether; the rest of the app works without them.
